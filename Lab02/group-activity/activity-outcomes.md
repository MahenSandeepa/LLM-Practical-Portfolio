# Group 8 – Product Development Outcomes

## Product Name

**SpendWise AI**

**Tagline:** Your Money. Your Goals. Smarter Decisions.

## Product Concept

SpendWise AI is a proposed AI-powered mobile budgeting application designed specifically for university students. It aims to help students understand their spending habits, monitor daily expenses, manage limited budgets, and make informed financial decisions.

Students would be able to record expenses, set budgets, monitor spending patterns, and receive personalised financial suggestions through an AI assistant.

## Key Features

1. **Smart Expense Tracker:** Records and categorises daily expenses such as food, transportation, education, shopping, and entertainment.

2. **AI-Powered Budget Planner:** Suggests realistic spending limits based on students' available money, recurring expenses, and savings goals.

3. **Smart Spending Alerts:** Warns students when they approach or exceed their spending limits.

4. **AI Financial Assistant:** Allows students to ask budgeting questions and receive personalised spending suggestions.

5. **Savings Goals and Challenges:** Helps students establish financial goals and encourages better saving habits.

6. **Weekly Spending Insights:** Provides summaries of spending patterns and highlights categories where expenses are increasing.

## Target Users

The product is primarily designed for university students, particularly:

- Students receiving weekly or monthly allowances.
- Students earning income through part-time employment.
- Students living away from home and managing independent expenses.
- Students learning to manage personal budgets.
- Students saving for educational or personal goals.
