# SpendWise AI – Smart Student Budget Assistant

## Group Information

**Group:** 8

**Assigned Challenge:** The Budget-Challenged Student

## Problem Statement

University students frequently spend money on food, transportation, subscriptions, printing, shopping, and social activities. These small expenses can accumulate quickly, making it difficult for students to understand their spending habits and manage their limited budgets.

## Proposed Product

SpendWise AI is a proposed AI-powered budgeting application designed to help university students track expenses, monitor their remaining budgets, and make informed spending decisions.

The application would allow students to record daily expenses, view spending summaries, and ask an AI assistant for personalised budgeting suggestions.

## Example Scenario

A student receives a weekly allowance of Rs. 5,000 and has already spent Rs. 3,200.

The student asks:

"Can I spend Rs. 1,000 on dinner tonight?"

SpendWise AI would show that only Rs. 800 would remain after the purchase and encourage the student to consider upcoming essential expenses.

## Intended Outcome

The proposed product aims to help students understand where their money goes, control unnecessary spending, and manage their allowances more effectively.
