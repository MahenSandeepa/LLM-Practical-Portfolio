# LLM-Assisted Product Development

## Group Activity

Group 8 – The Budget-Challenged Student

## LLM Used

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## Prompt 1 – Product Concept Development

**Purpose:** To develop the group's proposed SpendWise AI concept by exploring the problem, product functionality, key features, and target users.

**Outcome:** ChatGPT provided a structured product concept for an AI-powered student budgeting application, including expense tracking, budget planning, spending alerts, an AI financial assistant, savings goals, and weekly insights.

## Prompt 2 – Visual Prototype Generation

**Purpose:** To generate a visual mock-up based on the developed product concept and its features.

**Outcome:** ChatGPT generated a mobile application mock-up illustrating four main screens: Home Dashboard, Expense Tracker, AI Assistant, and Savings Goals / Budget Planner.

## Summary

The two-prompt process demonstrates the use of ChatGPT for product concept development and visual prototyping. The resulting product proposal addresses the Group 8 challenge through student-focused budgeting features and personalised spending guidance.
